# Domain Decision Explanations

The **Decision Center** explains why a domain was allowed or blocked instead of showing only the final result. You can open an explanation from the Activity or Live views, or inspect a domain manually.

An explanation includes:

- The final allow or block decision.
- The decision layer, such as a custom list, category, keyword rule, or security pack.
- The matching rule or source.
- A low, review, or high risk level.
- A practical recommendation based on the result.

The Decision Center also provides rule simulation and conflict detection. Simulating a rule does not save or activate it; it shows the expected effect before you make a real change.

Risk levels are guidance based on locally available rules. They are not a guarantee that a website is safe or malicious.
