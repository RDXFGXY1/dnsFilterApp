# Discipline: Needs Attention

Open **Discipline → Needs Attention** to see overdue work, tasks due today,
reminders, upcoming work, unplanned tasks, and blocked work in one place.
Filters let you narrow the view without changing the underlying tasks.

The Rescue Plan control accepts an available time window from 15 minutes to
2 hours and suggests up to three actionable tasks. Waiting or blocked work is
left out of the suggestion. The plan is a local planning aid; review the task
details and deadlines before changing your schedule.
